About Me

Stories have always fascinated me.

As a journalist, I spent years sitting across from farmers, entrepreneurs, community leaders, and ordinary people with extraordinary stories. I learned that behind every successful project, thriving business, or transformed life is someone who simply needed the opportunity to be seen and heard.

Those conversations shaped the way I write today.

Now, I help Christian coaches, authors, and course creators tell their own stories so the people God has called them to serve can find them.

I know that running a purpose-driven business means wearing many hats. One day you're writing an email, the next you're recording a course, editing videos, designing a workbook, posting on social media, and trying to remember where you saved your latest Canva file. Somewhere in between, you're also showing up for your family, your clients, and your ministry.

It can be overwhelming, and that's where I come in.

I enjoy taking ideas that exist only as notes, voice recordings, or unfinished drafts and turning them into content that's clear, engaging, and true to your voice. Whether it's a launch email sequence, a lead magnet, a course workbook, a social media campaign, or a polished video, I love bringing all the pieces together so you can focus on what you do best.

My background in journalism taught me to ask good questions, listen carefully, and tell stories that matter. Over the years, that naturally grew into helping businesses communicate more clearly through content, email marketing, video editing, and creative support.

Today, PurposePath Media brings all of those experiences together.

I partner with Christian business owners who want more than polished content, but want someone who understands the heart behind their work, and someone who cares as much about their message as the marketing because good content isn't about saying more. It's about saying the right things in a way that genuinely serves people.

I also believe business can be a way to extend God's work beyond ourselves. That's why a portion of every project supports ministry initiatives here in the Philippines. Every website launched, every email sent, every video edited, and every piece of content published becomes part of something much bigger than a business transaction.

If you're looking for someone who can help carry the creative load while treating your mission with the same care you do, I'd be honored to work with you.

Let's create content with purpose and use it to make an impact that lasts.
